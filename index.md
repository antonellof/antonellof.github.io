👋 Hello world.

I'm an AI Cloud Engineer and Architect interested mostly in LLM inference, Agentic AI and Cloud.

My open-source projects include [Cognitora inference](https://github.com/antonellof/cognitora-inference) (KV-aware orchestration above vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM and llama.cpp, on bare metal, Kubernetes or cloud), [Frink](https://github.com/antonellof/frink) (a pure-Rust GGUF engine, checked against llama.cpp on 100 architectures), [GPUlayer](https://www.gpulayer.dev/) (runs PyTorch workloads on any GPU you can reach, from a laptop to cloud VMs created per job), and two research repos: [LASA](https://github.com/antonellof/lineage-attack-surface-analyzer) (jailbreak transfer from base models to fine-tunes) and [moe-copilot](https://github.com/antonellof/moe-copilot) (MoE routing imbalance cost in Megatron-LM).

This blog is where I write about cloud architecture, inference, GPUs, distributed systems and engineering leadership.

🕹️ Learned to code on a Commodore, and I've basically been hitting RUN ever since.
